| T-20-02 | Information Disclosure | source/readiness DTO fields | medium | mitigate | use bounded reason codes and typed summaries; never place source URLs, secrets, signed material or raw runner payload in the contract |
| T-20-03 | Elevation of Privilege | movie_source_state relation | medium | mitigate | key current projection by existing `movie.id`, retain existing admin/R18 route guards, and add no caller-controlled content identity |
